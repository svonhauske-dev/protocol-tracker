Privacy Policy

Effective July 6, 2026

Origin (“the app”) is a personal supplement and medication tracker. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account info — your email address and a display name you provide, to create and access your account.
  • Your tracking data — the supplements and medications you add, doses, schedules, protocols, and which items you’ve checked off each day.
  • A user identifier associated with your account.
  • Product-analytics events — which features you use and screens you view, tied to your account identifier, to understand usage and improve the app.
  • A push token — if you turn on reminders, a device push token so we can send your dose and refill notifications.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

To provide the app’s functionality — storing your account, syncing your supplements and daily logs, showing your schedule, and sending the reminders you enable — and to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. We do not use your data for advertising.

Where it’s stored

Your account and tracking data are stored securely with our backend provider (Supabase) over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in token is stored locally on your device. When reminders are enabled, your schedule and push token are stored on our server so notifications can be delivered to your device via Apple Push Notification service.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with providers acting on our behalf to run the app: Supabase (storing and serving your data), PostHog (product analytics), and Apple / Expo (delivering push notifications). We do not share your data with any other third parties.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your supplements and data in the app at any time. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Children

Origin is not directed to children under 13.
